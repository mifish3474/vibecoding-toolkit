Step 2: Guardrails

Privacy is a design decision.

Before you build, ask what information your tool truly needs — and whether it needs student information at all.

Five questions to ask

  • Can I build and test this with fictional names, seat numbers, or sample data?
  • Will the tool collect, display, save, or transmit student information?
  • Could a student be identified from the information, even without a name?
  • Where does information go, who can access it, and how long does it remain?
  • Do district policies permit this AI service and this use of student information?

Avoid uploading identifiable student records to an unapproved AI platform. An AI tool is not automatically FERPA-compliant simply because it is popular, district-accessible, or uses local storage.

Simple first projects are ideal

A timer, supplies calculator, directions display, or scoreboard can often be built without any student data. When your tool starts using real education records, pause and seek district guidance before deployment.
