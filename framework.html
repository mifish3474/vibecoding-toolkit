Your roadmap

Six steps. One useful tool.

Vibe coding works best when you start with a problem rather than a list of features.

01

Identify

What specific classroom frustration are you trying to solve? Start with a small, real problem.

02

Guardrails

What information needs protection? Consider FERPA, district policies, data collection, storage, and sharing before design.

03

Describe

Explain in everyday language what the tool should do and what a successful first version looks like.

04

Build

Let your AI partner create the simplest working version. You do not need to understand every line of code.

05

Test & Refine

Try it in the environment where you will actually use it. Report what works, what breaks, and what to change.

06

Share

Decide whether and how to publish. Check access, privacy, functionality, and device compatibility first.

Keep the first version small. A simple tool that works is more valuable than a complicated tool that almost works. The starter prompt will guide you through these steps conversationally.
